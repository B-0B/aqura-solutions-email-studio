# Get Found Online — Campaign Plan

**Campaign:** Get Found Online
**Goal:** 40 free consultation calls booked in 4 weeks
**Channel:** Email only (no paid ads)
**Audience:** ~500 businesses on our contact list
**Timeline:** December 7 – January 1, 2027 (4 weeks)

---

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Summary:** A four-week email campaign inviting businesses on our contact list to book a free consultation about websites and apps that help them reach more customers.

**Primary objective:** Book 40 free consultation calls by January 1, 2027 (8% conversion from 500 contacts).

**Secondary objectives:**
- Re-engage contacts who haven't heard from us in a while
- Surface upsell opportunities with existing clients
- Learn which segment responds best so we can plan future campaigns

---

## 2. Target Audience

Three segments, all from our existing contact list (~500 total).

### Segment A: Startups
Founders and early-stage teams building their first real online presence. They need to get in front of customers but don't have an in-house dev team. They care about cost, speed, and looking credible from day one.

### Segment B: Established Businesses
Companies with an existing website or app that isn't pulling its weight. They know they need to improve but aren't sure where to start or who to trust. They care about reliability, clear communication, and not wasting budget.

### Segment C: Existing Clients
Businesses we've already worked with. They trust us but may not know about services we've added since their last project. They care about continuity, expanding what they already have, and working with people who already understand their business.

**Buying stage:** Consideration to decision. These are warm contacts — they already know who we are.

---

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** "We build websites and apps that help your business reach more customers — let's talk about what that looks like for you."

### Supporting messages (by segment)

| Segment | Message | Proof point |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Startups | "You've got the idea — we'll help you build something your first customers can find and use." | Reference past startup projects we've shipped quickly. |
| Established | "If your website isn't bringing in customers the way it should, a focused rebuild can change that." | Before-and-after examples of sites we've improved. |
| Existing clients | "We've built together before — here's what we can do next." | Specific services they haven't used yet. |

### Voice check (from brand/voice.md)
- Use "we," "together," "let's," "your vision"
- Plain verbs: build, ship, fix, improve, launch
- No buzzwords, no jargon, no pushy CTAs
- Never promise specific results (traffic, revenue, conversions)
- Sign off campaign emails with: "Warm regards, The Aqura Solutions Team"

---

## 4. Channel Strategy

**Channel:** Email (owned)

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Why email | Our audience is already on our list. Email is free to send, personal, and lets us segment messaging. No budget needed beyond the tools we already have. |
| Format | Plain-text-style HTML emails (simple, clean, no heavy design). Feels like a note from a real person, not a marketing blast. |
| Effort | Low to medium. Writing and scheduling 12 emails over 4 weeks. |
| Budget | Near zero — uses our existing email platform. |

**Why not other channels:** The brief specifies email only, small budget. This is the right constraint — the list is warm, and a focused email sequence will outperform scattershot outreach.

## 7. Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to track | Cadence |
|--------|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Consultations booked** (primary KPI) | 40 | Booking page / calendar tool | Daily |
| Email open rate | 35%+ | Email platform | After each send |
| Email click-through rate | 5%+ | Email platform | After each send |
| Booking page conversion rate | 20%+ of clicks | Booking tool + email platform | Weekly |
| Unsubscribe rate | Below 1% per send | Email platform | After each send |
| Consultations completed | 30+ (75% show rate) | Calendar / CRM | Weekly |

### Weekly check-in
Every Monday, review the previous week's numbers. If a segment is underperforming:
- Open rate low: test a new subject line on the next send
- Click rate low: simplify the email body, make the CTA more prominent
- Booking rate low: check the booking page for friction

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Holiday timing (Weeks 3–4)** | Lower open rates during Christmas/New Year week. People are away. | Keep Dec 23 and Dec 28 emails short and low-pressure. Front-load the campaign — aim for 25+ bookings by end of Week 2. |
| **List fatigue** | 500 contacts is a small list. Over-emailing could spike unsubscribes. | Cap at one email per segment per week (plus one bonus). Watch unsubscribe rate closely. If any send exceeds 1.5% unsubs, skip the next scheduled send for that segment. |
| **Low booking page conversion** | People click but don't book — friction in the booking flow. | Test the booking page before launch. Keep it to one page, one action. Offer 2–3 time slots per day so there's always something convenient. |
