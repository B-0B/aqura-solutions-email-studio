# Email Studio

This project is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- **brand/** — Brand voice guidelines, tone references, and style notes.
- **leads/** — Audience segments and contact list descriptions (no real email addresses).
- **campaigns/** — Drafts, outlines, and final copy for email campaigns.
- **notes/** — Research, ideas, and working notes.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask before deleting anything.
