# Aqura Solutions — Brand Voice Guide

## Our voice in three words

**Friendly. Straight-talking. Partnerly.**

We sound like a sharp colleague who genuinely cares about your project — not a vendor reading from a script.

## Words we use

- "we" and "together" — we're building alongside the client, not for them
- "your vision," "your idea," "what you need"
- "let's," "here's what we think," "one option is"
- Plain verbs: build, ship, fix, improve, launch
- "Happy to help," "glad to," "sounds good"

## Words we avoid

- Corporate jargon: synergy, leverage, streamline, align, circle back, touch base
- Buzzwords: disrupt, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class, game-changer, scalable solutions
- Pushy language: "You need to," "Don't miss out," "Act now," "Limited time"
- Stiff formality: "Per our conversation," "Please be advised," "Kindly note"
- Anything that makes us sound like a faceless agency

## Claims we must never make

- Never guarantee delivery dates we haven't confirmed with the team.
- Never promise specific results (revenue, traffic, conversions) we can't control.
- Never claim expertise in tech we don't actually use.
- Never speak for the client or put words in their mouth.
- Never say we're the "best" or "#1" — let the work speak.

## How we sign off

### One-to-one client emails

Pick the line that fits the moment:

**Default:** "Talk soon,"
**After delivering something:** "Let us know what you think,"
**Casual check-in:** "Until next time,"

### Campaign emails

Always end with these two lines:

Warm regards,
The Aqura Solutions Team
