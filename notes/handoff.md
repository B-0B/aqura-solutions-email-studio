# Handoff: Angle A vs. Angle B

**What each group cares about:** Startups want credibility and speed. Established businesses want a site that matches how far they've come. Existing clients want to build on what we've already done together.

**Angle A** (being found by more customers) speaks to the outcome — more customers finding you. Appeals most to startups and established businesses who feel invisible online.

**Angle B** (free, no-pressure conversation) speaks to the offer — low risk, high trust. Appeals most to established businesses who are cautious, and existing clients who just need a reason to re-engage.

**Pick:** Angle B. Our list is warm — these people already know us. They don't need to be sold on *why* visibility matters; they need a reason to take the next step. A friendly, no-pressure call is that reason. Angle A is a stronger hook for cold outreach or ads, not for people who are already on our list.
